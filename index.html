A clinician with a founder’s mindset.

Abdul-Rahman was born on 5 March 2002 on Zanzibar Island, Tanzania. He trained as a Clinical Medical Assistant in India and now works in patient care in Tanzania.

Outside the clinic he runs ZanLux, a fragrance shop in Dodoma. Medicine taught him discipline and empathy; business taught him to build something that lasts.
